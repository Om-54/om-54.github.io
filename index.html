WORKFLOW METHODOLOGY

HOW I BUILD CONTENT

A repeatable 6-step content pipeline balancing creative experimentation with rigorous delivery.

01

RESEARCH

Understand audience, platform and context. Identify what themes are currently capturing attention, study audience comments, and analyze whitespace opportunities.

02

IDEATE

Generate concepts, hooks and content angles. Develop 5 to 10 hook variations per concept to find the angle that stops feed scrolling within 1.5 seconds.

03

CREATE

Develop video, graphics and platform-specific assets. Edit with intentional rhythm, dynamic typography overlays, sound synchronization, and brand cohesion.

04

PUBLISH

Adapt content to the intended platform and format. Optimize video aspect ratios, craft contextual captions, test thumbnail packaging, and apply targeted keywords.

05

REVIEW

Observe available feedback and performance signals. Review watch-time retention curves, qualitative comments, saves, and shares to understand viewer behavior.

06

ITERATE

Improve future content based on what was learned. Refine pacing where drop-offs occurred, spin off top comments into new videos, and double down on winning hooks.
